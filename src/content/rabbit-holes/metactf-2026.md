Another CTF, another day of staring at terminals and questioning my life choices.

This time, I participated in **MetaCTF 2026** at Princess Sumaya University for Technology with my team, **No Shelter**.

![MetaCTF 2026 venue](/images/rabbit-holes/metactf-2026/stagepic.JPG)

We spent the day jumping between challenges, exploring different ideas, and keeping way too many browser tabs open.

I spent a good amount of time digging into web challenges, messing with requests, endpoints, and whatever looked suspicious.

The server seemed particularly fond of `403 Forbidden`.

## The One That Got Away

Near the end of the competition, I was working on a reverse engineering challenge.

And somehow, in the very last minute, I managed to get what looked like most of the flag.

Just one little piece left.

![Almost had the flag](/images/rabbit-holes/metactf-2026/lastflagpic.jpg)

Then the competition ended.

The instance closed.

And that was it. 🫠

## Not Bad, I Guess

We finished **17th with 1,767 points**, and I contributed 552 of them.

![Our MetaCTF result](/images/rabbit-holes/metactf-2026/result.png)

Didn't win, but I had fun, learned a few things, and walked away with another CTF experience.

Oh, and I got a free shirt. So that's a win, I guess.

**Until the next one.**
